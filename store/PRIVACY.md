# Privacy Policy: Gradescope+

Gradescope+ runs only on gradescope.com.

- It reads your Gradescope course pages, using your existing login session, to find assignment due dates, submission status and scores.
- It stores that data only in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`) as a 10-minute cache.
- If you enter category weights for a course, it stores them in the same place until you remove them.
- Syllabus text you paste is read on your computer to fill in weights. It is not stored or sent anywhere.
- "Copy a prompt for an AI chat" only puts a prompt, with your course's assignment names, on your clipboard. Where you paste it is up to you; the extension sends nothing.
- It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. It has no analytics and no remote servers.
- Removing the extension deletes the cached data and any saved weights.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/RakuReach/gradescope/issues
